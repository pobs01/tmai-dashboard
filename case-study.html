THE CHALLENGE

Client Data Can't Touch the Public Cloud

warning

Sensitive Ad Accounts: Managing Google Ads for 20+ clients means handling campaign data, budget information, and performance metrics that competitors would pay to see.

warning

AI-Powered Workflows: Using AI for ad copy generation, campaign analysis, and reporting means feeding client data into LLMs — a non-starter with public cloud APIs.

warning

POPIA Compliance: South Africa's data protection law requires explicit consent for cross-border data transfers. Cloud AI APIs violate this by design.
